本项目保留所使用开源组件的来源与许可证. 如认为代码、文档或图稿涉及您的权利, 请通过本仓库 Issues 提供具体链接、相关权利信息和希望采取的处理方式. 维护者会核实并配合合理的补充署名、修订或移除请求.

Please use this repository's Issues to identify the specific material, your relevant rights and the requested remedy. The maintainer will investigate and cooperate with appropriate attribution, correction or removal. Upstream licenses and notices remain applicable.
